## 2. Information Coefficient (IC) Breakdown (The Bad News)

While the portfolio made a massive amount of money, we need to analyze *why* it made money. To do that, the backtester measures the **Information Coefficient (IC)** of every sub-model. The IC tells us whether a model's predictions correctly ranked the forward returns of the stocks.

Here are the Information Ratios (ICIR) by model at the 5-day predictive horizon:

| Model | IC (5-day) | ICIR (5-day) | Hit Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mean Reversion** | +0.297 | **2.088** | 100% |
| **Momentum** | -0.030 | -0.095 | 42.9% |
| **Vol Timing** | -0.020 | -0.120 | 42.9% |
| **ML Model (XGBoost/LR/RF)**| -0.073 | **-0.734** | 28.6% |

> [!WARNING]
> **ML Assessment:** **FAILING**
> Despite the `ml_pipeline` reporting a massive 0.61 out-of-sample AUC during training, the strict walk-forward backtest reveals that the **ML model actually possessed a NEGATIVE Information Coefficient** (-0.073 IC, -0.734 ICIR). 

### What does this mean?
The incredible portfolio returns (+123.8%) were almost entirely generated by the traditional `mean_reversion` factor (which possessed a staggering 2.088 ICIR) combined with the Black-Litterman allocator's intelligent risk-weighting! The ML predictions actually *dragged down* the performance slightly.

## Conclusion & Next Steps

**Is it a good model to use?**
No, not yet. The ML model is likely heavily overfitting to the specific validation holdouts during the training phase, which is a classic trap in financial machine learning (often caused by overlapping returns, non-stationary regimes, or features that don't generalize cross-sectionally).

**How to fix it:**
If we want the ML engine to genuinely contribute positive alpha to the portfolio alongside the traditional factors, we need to address the overfit. I highly recommend we pivot to integrating those **Alternative Data** sources we brainstormed (like off-exchange Dark Pool Short Volume or Options flow). Providing the tree models with distinct, uncorrelated alternative features is the most proven way to break the curse of price-feature overfitting.
