LEDGER GUIDE RULES
  • Buy / Sell — Enter price per share, excluding fees. Log the fee separately via Quick Fee Log.
  • Deposit — Total cash amount added to account. Ticker auto-sets to CASH.
  • Dividend — Total cash received. Ticker auto-sets to CASH.
  • Fee — Use the Quick Fee Log below for speed. Deducts from cash.
  • After logging — Click ⟳ REFRESH to recalculate the engine and update all metrics.
  • Fractional shares — Quantity supports up to 6 decimal places (Trade Republic precision).
  • Auto-refresh — Dashboard refreshes display every 10 seconds. Engine recalculates only on manual ⟳ REFRESH.
